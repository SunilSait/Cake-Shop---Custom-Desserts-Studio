Our Process

Our Bespoke Commission Roadmap

A structured, collaborative journey tailored to ensure every aesthetic and flavor detail is executed flawlessly.

1. Submit Design Brief

Complete our online brief with your celebration date, guest count, theme palette, venue details, and reference images.

2. Sketch & Flavor Profile

Our pastry design team creates an architectural scale sketch and recommends tailored flavor pairings for your review.

3. Approval & Tasting

Sample flavor harmonies in our studio or tasting box, finalize dimensions, and confirm your commission with a deposit.

4. Handcraft & Delivery

We bake and assemble your centerpiece 24 hours prior, followed by refrigerated white-glove transport and on-site setup.

Preparation Guide

What to Include in Your Brief

Providing the following information helps our pastry artists provide an accurate quote and sketch promptly.

Celebration type (wedding reception, anniversary, milestone gala, corporate launch)
Exact guest count and portion preference (dessert size vs. coffee portions)
Desired cake sponge flavor, filling cream, and frosting style
Color palette, stationery swatches, or floral theme inspiration
Reference imagery, moodboard links, or Pinterest boards
Target budget range for dessert design and staging
Venue address, delivery access time, and event coordinator contact
Specific dietary specifications (Gluten-Free, Vegan, Nut-Free, Dairy-Free)
