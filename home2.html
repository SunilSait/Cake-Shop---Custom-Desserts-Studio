Creation Process

From Vision to Unforgettable Reveal

Every bespoke creation follows our structured 4-stage culinary design methodology.

1

Design Brief & Consultation

Share your event aesthetic, moodboard, guest count, and flavor preferences through our online brief or private in-studio appointment.

2

Architectural Sketch & Palette

Our head pastry artist renders a scale architectural sketch and curates a personalized flavor pairing profile for your approval.

3

Scratch-Baking & Handcrafting

Using imported French butter and single-origin cocoa, our pastry chefs handcraft your layers, sugar flowers, and structural details.

4

White-Glove Transport & Setup

Refrigerated white-glove transport ensures your cake arrives in pristine condition, professionally assembled on-site at your venue.
